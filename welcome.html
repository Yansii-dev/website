Manage your subscription

Manage, cancel, or update payment:

  1. Open the email from Dodo Payments and click "Access License" — or go directly to customer.dodopayments.com
  2. Enter the email you used at checkout
  3. Dodo sends you a login link — click it to open the customer portal
  4. From the portal you can:
  • Cancel your subscription
  • Change payment method
  • Download invoices and billing history
  • Re-download your license key
When you cancel, Pro stays active through the end of your current billing period. After that, the extension reverts to the Free tier — your findings and data are never deleted.
