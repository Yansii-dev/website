Activation

How to Activate YANSII Pro

1

Find your license key

Check your email for a message from Dodo Payments. It contains your license key (a long string like xxxxxxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxxxxxxxxxx). You can also find it anytime by logging into the Dodo customer portal at customer.dodopayments.com

2

Open the YANSII extension

Click the YANSII icon in your Chrome toolbar. You'll see the Free tier with a license key input field.

3

Paste and activate

Paste your license key into the field and click Activate. The extension validates your key with our server.

4

You're Pro

That's it. You'll see "PRO Active — renews [date]" at the top. All features are unlocked.

All Pro features unlocked
💡 Tip: Use a dedicated Chrome profile for hunting

Create a separate Chrome profile for bug bounty testing. This keeps your hunting sessions isolated from personal browsing — YANSII won't scan your email, banking, or social media by accident.

How to set up:

  1. Click your profile icon (top-right of Chrome)
  2. Click "Add" to create a new profile
  3. Name it "Bug Bounty" or "Hunting"
  4. Install YANSII from the Chrome Web Store in this profile
  5. Paste your license key — Pro works on up to 3 devices or profiles

For role-based testing, create additional profiles: "Target - Admin" (logged in as admin), "Target - User" (logged in as regular user). Then use YANSII's Role Comparison to diff what each can access.

Troubleshooting

Didn't get the email?
Check spam. Still nothing? Log into customer.dodopayments.com to re-download your key. Or email hello@yansii.in
Activation failed?
Make sure you paste the full key with no extra spaces. Try clicking Test after pasting to check connectivity.
Switching devices?
Your license works on up to 3 devices. Just paste the same key on each one.
