Pricing FAQ

Quick answers

+Is the free tier really free forever?

Yes. No trial, no feature countdown, no credit card. The 46-scanner passive engine, dashboard, and exports stay free indefinitely.

+When can I actually buy Pro?

Not yet — Pro is still being finished. The buy button above will connect to checkout the moment it's ready. Join the notify list and you'll hear first.

+What does the 14-day trial include?

Full Pro access — every feature, no restrictions — for 14 days once you start. No credit card required to begin the trial.

+Why isn't the AI cost included in the $19?

Because you choose your own AI provider and model — some are cheaper, some are free-tier eligible, and you keep full control of your usage and spend. Bundling it in would mean paying for AI you might not use, or a markup on usage you could get cheaper directly.

+Is India pricing final?

₹999/month and ₹8,999/year are the planned India rates, set to reflect local purchasing power rather than a straight currency conversion.
